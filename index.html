The Digital Perspective is an evolving visual practice by Zarir Madon — where photography becomes abstraction, technology becomes a creative instrument, and ordinary subjects are pushed somewhere stranger.

The work moves freely between fine art, graphic design, surrealism and digital experimentation. There are no strict borders. Only interesting ones to cross.
