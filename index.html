About the Artist

Zarir Madon

Zarir Madon is a visual artist, photographer, and designer based in Auckland, New Zealand. His creative practice spans photography, fine art prints, and modern graphic design, focusing on clarity, balance, and quiet visual impact.

Whether working on creative commissions, original artwork, or design projects, Zarir emphasizes thoughtful composition and timeless aesthetic quality.
